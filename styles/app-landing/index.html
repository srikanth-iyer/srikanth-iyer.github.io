A model is a story you can run; a story is a model you can feel.

Ethos

Net positive?

Does this leave the people affected by it better off — including the ones who never get a vote?

Honest at the boundaries?

Clear about what the model assumes and where it stops being trustworthy.

Legible to the governed?

Could the people inside this system understand the rules it runs on?

The Weatherwax test

Sin is treating people as things. That covers most of applied ethics.
