## Overview

Modeling isn't neutral. I care about using these tools to design mechanisms and policies that are a net positive for the people inside them — and about being honest regarding what models can and cannot tell us.

## The questions I ask of any model

- **Net positive?** Does this leave the people affected by it better off, including the ones who never get a vote?
- **Honest at the boundaries?** Am I clear about what the model assumes and where it stops being trustworthy?
- **Legible to the governed?** Could the people inside this system understand the rules it runs on?
