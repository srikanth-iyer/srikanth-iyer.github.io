## Overview

How do distributed systems with no central controller end up performing computation? I'm interested in the conditions under which collective behavior becomes information processing — in cellular automata, swarms, and economies alike.

The density classification task is the toy version of a deep question: how does a system with no global view compute a global fact? The same shape of question recurs in markets, ecosystems, and language.

## Questions

1. When does local interaction become genuine information processing?
2. What structures (particles, domains, signals) carry computation in spatially extended systems?
3. How do these ideas transfer from cellular automata to economic and social systems?
